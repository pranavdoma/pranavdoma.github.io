Hi, I'm Pranav

Perception, compute, and control: the holy trinity, as far as I'm concerned. The interesting part is getting all three to work together on a real system, with real limits on latency, safety, and compute. I like building things end to end, then figuring out why they behave differently on hardware than they did on paper.

I completed my Master's in Computer Engineering at New York University, where I worked on robotics, planning, and learning-enabled control. Before that, I studied Electrical Engineering at SVNIT Surat.

I'm a robotics generalist at HCLTech America, working across autonomous systems and robotics. A lot of the work is getting the pieces to fit, then finding out where they don't once they meet real-world constraints.

I'm part of Autoware's VisionPilot project. The project is on GitHub, and you can browse my Autoware contributions there too.

Sports are a year-round thing for me: NFL Sundays for the Bears and Giants, and when MLB season rolls around, I'm rooting for the Yankees and Cubs. I'm always up for cricket, tennis, or volleyball too. Add pizza, a good movie, or a rabbit hole about geopolitics or deep tech and I'm set.
