About

I work on autonomous systems in which perception, planning, and control have to hold together on a real machine. Latency, safety, and compute are part of the problem. I am a robotics engineer at HCLTech America, and I completed an M.S. in Computer Engineering at New York University. At NYU I was an assistant research scientist in the Agile Safe Autonomous Systems Lab, working on language-guided planning and agentic vehicle control.

The work I care about now sits between end-to-end driving and simulation that keeps metric geometry. In the Autoware Foundation I develop VisionPilot, an open-source Level-2 assistance stack that estimates the road from a single front camera. With collaborators at HCLTech I coauthored SynAirG, a geometry-first generator of bronchoscopy worlds, and gave the spotlight talk at the IROS 2026 SurgTwin workshop. Earlier papers, with the NYU lab, study agentic control of high-dynamic maneuvers and planners that accept instructions in natural language.

Research interests

  • End-to-end driving and perception that fit on vehicle hardware
  • Simulation substrates whose geometry stays consistent with the source scan
  • Language and agentic models used as controllers, checked against physics
  • Humanoid teleoperation, and the data it produces for later learning
